## Goal

---
# Artificial Intelligence (AI)
AI refers to machines performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.
Examples:
- ChatGPT
- Microsoft Copilot
- Autonomous Vehicles
---
# Machine Learning (ML)
ML is a subset of AI.
Machines learn patterns from data without being explicitly programmed.
Examples:
- Fraud Detection
- Recommendation Systems
- Credit Risk Assessment
---
# Deep Learning
Deep Learning uses Neural Networks with multiple layers.
Examples:
- Image Recognition
- Speech Recognition
- Natural Language Processing
---
# Generative AI
Generative AI creates new content.
Examples:
- Text
- Images
- Code
- Audio
Popular Models:
- GPT
- Claude
- Gemini
---
# Agentic AI
Agentic AI can:
- Plan
- Reason
- Take Actions
Examples:
- AI Scheduling Assistant
- Incident Response Agent
- Multi-Agent Systems
